## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intersection of cutting-edge energy intelligence, environmental impact, and industrial-grade IoT telemetry. It is calibrated to serve two interconnected operational tiers: an intuitive, high-clarity Consumer Dashboard delivering personal consumption metrics and grid savings, and a dense, rigorous Operations Command Center engineered for infrastructure analysts, grid engineers, and facility supervisors.

The visual direction follows **Technical Minimalism with Tactile Precision**. It delivers an analytical, scientific aesthetic without sacrificing human readability. UI layers utilize crisp white and pale slate foundations, accented by vivid emerald and deep forest greens that reinforce sustainable energy conversion. Data density is achieved through clear micro-borders, purposeful tabular layouts, monospace-accented numerical callouts, and low-noise telemetry status pills. The interface inspires confidence, high reliability, and real-time responsiveness.

## Colors

The palette balances clinical precision with high-contrast chromatic signals to communicate energy states instantly.

- **Primary (`#10B981` / `#059669`):** Represents optimal operational states, active energy flow, peak generation, and confirmed automated savings.
- **Secondary (`#064E3B` / `#022C22`):** Grounding forest greens providing authoritative structural weight, applied to operational headers, dense metric titles, active navigation anchors, and executive summaries.
- **Tertiary (`#0284C7`):** Precision technical cyan-blue designated for secondary telemetry readouts, HVAC airflow dynamics, and predictive algorithmic forecasting.
- **Neutrals (`#0F172A` down to `#F8FAFC`):** Slate-derived dark neutrals deliver high-legibility typographic contrast (`#0F172A` for primary data labels and body; `#64748B` for secondary units and timestamps), layered over clean, non-glare white and light slate surfaces.
- **Safety & Diagnostic Alarms:** `#F59E0B` flags load-limit anomalies and dynamic peak pricing warnings; `#EF4444` is reserved strictly for hardware faults, line disconnects, and safety tripping.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs **Geist** and **Inter** to construct an analytical, high-density environment.

- **Display & Headings:** Geist delivers sharp, geometric proportions with condensed tracking at large scales, communicating engineering rigor across facility statistics, device titles, and dashboard view headers.
- **Telemetry Readouts (`metric-display`, `telemetry-mono`):** Numerical wattages, kWh totals, and real-time voltage measurements utilize Geist with tabular numeral settings (`tnum`, `cv01`) to eliminate character jitter during rapid WebSocket telemetry pushes.
- **Body & Longform:** Inter serves as the foundation for documentation, fault descriptions, system logs, and actionable optimization insights, offering balanced horizontal cadence and superior multi-screen readability.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout architecture employs a dual-density model adapting directly to the operational context:

- **Consumer Dashboard:** Structured on a balanced 12-column fluid grid system bounded by a max container width of 1440px. Gutters remain comfortable (`1.5rem`), emphasizing breathing room between energy charts, household circuit segments, and monetary saving summaries.
- **ZeroWatt Operations Command Center:** Configured for maximum telemetry density across multi-monitor or widescreen workstations. Columns break into 16-to-24 modular grid segments with reduced gutters (`0.75rem` to `1rem`) and edge-to-edge canvas margins (`1.5rem`), facilitating synchronous multi-feed monitoring of sub-stations, transformer relays, and battery reserves.
- **Responsive Adaptation:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Collapses into a single 4-column flow. Layout shifts to vertical metric cards; telemetry charts switch from continuous brush charts to high-level sparklines.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout. Command center panels shift to an accordion or segmented master-detail stack.
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** Full 12/16-column matrix with persistent collapsible telemetry sidebars and high-throughput data grids.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects heavy skeuomorphism and blurry, unfocused drop shadows in favor of **Low-Contrast Outlines & Tonal Surface Stacking**:

- **Baseline Level 0 (Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` provides a calm, glare-free working floor for intense data analysis.
- **Card Level 1 (Panels & Widgets):** `#FFFFFF` solid fill framed with a crisp micro-border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`). Subtle ambient illumination is applied via `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Raised Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Segmented Modals):** Background `#FFFFFF` framed by `1px solid #CBD5E1` with an elevated ambient shadow `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.
- **Command Center Overlay Level 3 (Diagnostics & Filter Drawers):** Backdrop filter `blur(8px)` with semi-opaque wash (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`), bordered with `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)` and directional shadow `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design uses a **Rounded** shape model (`0.5rem` / `8px` base) that tempers technical rigor with accessible consumer appeal:

- **Metrics & Telemetry Panels:** Outer boundaries use `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) for clean visual containment without feeling boxy.
- **Sub-cards & Controls:** Form inputs, buttons, segmented toggles, and data tables employ `rounded` (`0.5rem` / `8px`), maintaining alignment across tight modular rows.
- **Pill Telemetry Badges:** Live connection indicators, wattage status tags, and diagnostic alarms feature full stadium rounding (`9999px`) to create clear semantic differentiation from interactive square buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid emerald green (`#10B981`) with white text (`#FFFFFF`), `font-weight: 600`, transition on hover to `#059669`. Micro-border: `1px solid rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.2)`. Padding: `0.5rem 1rem`.
- **Secondary / Command:** Slate border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`), background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`. On hover: background `#F8FAFC`, border color `#CBD5E1`.
- **Danger / Circuit Trip:** Red tint background (`#FEF2F2`), border `1px solid #FCA5A5`, text `#DC2626`. On hover: background `#EF4444`, text `#FFFFFF`.

### Chips & Telemetry Badges
- **Online / Normal:** Full pill radius, background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, text `#065F46`, paired with an animated pulse dot (`#10B981`) measuring `6px`.
- **Warning / Peak Demand:** Background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`, text `#92400E`, leading static amber indicator dot.
- **Fault / Offline:** Background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12)`, text `#991B1B`, leading red triangle icon.

### Cards
- **Consumer Metric Card:** Pure white background, `1rem` rounded corners, `1px solid #E2E8F0` border. Houses large Geist metric displays, subtext indicating percentage delta against weekly average, and an inline sparkline.
- **Operations Telemetry Node:** Condensed padding (`0.75rem`), dark slate title block with monospaced hardware ID, active load gauge, and live relay toggle switch.

### Inputs & Form Controls
- **Text & Numeric Inputs:** Height `40px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`. Active focus state triggers an emerald halo: `border-color: #10B981; box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `18px` square or circular inputs, checked state filled with `#10B981` displaying crisp white micro-icons. Unchecked states carry a distinct `#94A3B8` outline.

### Specialized Energy-Tech Components
- **Power Flow Diagram Canvas:** Scaled vector representation of energy exchange between Solar Inverter, Battery Pack, Main Grid, and Consumer Circuits. Animated dash strokes indicate active current flow in emerald (`#10B981`), while grid back-draw renders in amber (`#F59E0B`).
- **Phase Diagnostic Data Grid:** High-density, zebra-stripped table (`#FFFFFF` alternating with `#F8FAFC`), containing fixed column headers, right-aligned tabular numeric readouts for Voltage (V), Current (A), Frequency (Hz), and Power Factor (PF).
